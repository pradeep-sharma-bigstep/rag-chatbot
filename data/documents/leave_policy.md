# Acme Cloud — Leave Policy

Version 3.2, effective 1 January. Owner: People Operations (people@acmecloud.example).
This policy applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 5 unused days can be carried
over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

Employees who join part-way through the year receive annual leave pro rata from their start date, rounded up to the
nearest half day. Leave cannot be paid out in place of time off, except on termination of employment, when unused
accrued days are paid at the basic daily rate.

### Peak periods
Between 15 December and 2 January the support and operations teams run a reduced schedule. At most 40 percent of a
team may be on leave at the same time during this period. Requests are approved in the order they are submitted.

## Public holidays
Acme Cloud observes the public holidays of the country where the employee is based. If a public holiday falls on a
weekend, no replacement day is given. Employees who are required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day
off within 60 days, plus 1.5 times their daily rate if the work was on-call.

## Sick leave
Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for any sick leave longer than 2 consecutive working days.

Tell your manager before the start of your working day, or as soon as you are able. Sick days do not carry over.
If sick leave exceeds 10 days in a year, People Operations will contact you about extended medical leave, which is paid
at 60 percent of salary for up to 12 weeks.

## Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers receive 4 weeks. Parental leave can start up to 2 weeks before the
expected birth or adoption date.

Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Benefits and stock vesting continue during
leave. On return, employees are offered a phased schedule: 80 percent of hours at full pay for the first 4 weeks.

## Bereavement leave
Employees receive 5 paid days after the death of a spouse, partner, child, parent, or sibling, and 2 paid days for
other close relatives. Additional unpaid leave can be agreed with your manager.

## Study and volunteering leave
Each employee may take up to 3 paid days per year for approved study (exams, certifications) and 2 paid days for
volunteering with a registered charity. Both need manager approval at least 14 days ahead.

## Unpaid leave and sabbaticals
After 4 years of service, employees may apply for a sabbatical of 4 to 12 weeks unpaid. Applications go to the
department head at least 3 months in advance. Health insurance continues during a sabbatical.

## How to apply
Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 7 days in advance for annual
leave. Your manager approves or rejects the request within 3 working days.

For leave of more than 10 consecutive working days, submit the request at least 30 days in advance. If a request is
rejected, the manager must give a reason in the portal. Approved leave can be cancelled up to 48 hours before it starts.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can I take leave in the notice period?** Yes, but only with written agreement from your manager.

**Do carried-over days count before the new year's days?** Yes. Carried-over days are used first, so they do not
expire unused if you take leave before 31 March.

**Who do I contact about a mistake in my balance?** Email People Operations with a screenshot of the HR portal; they
answer within 2 working days.

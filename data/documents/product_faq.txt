Acme Cloud Storage — Frequently Asked Questions
Last updated: first quarter. Support: support@acmecloud.example

GENERAL

What is Acme Cloud Storage?
Acme Cloud Storage is an object storage service for files of any size. Data is
replicated across three availability zones.

Which regions are available?
Acme Cloud Storage runs in six regions: US East, US West, EU Central, EU West, Asia Pacific (Singapore) and Asia Pacific (Tokyo).
Data stays in the region you choose and is never moved unless you configure replication.

PRICING

How much does it cost?
The Standard tier costs 0.02 USD per GB per month. The Archive tier costs
0.004 USD per GB per month but retrieval takes up to 12 hours.

Are there charges for data transfer?
Uploading data is free. Downloading costs 0.05 USD per GB after the first 100 GB per month, which is free. Transfers
between buckets in the same region are free.

Is there a free tier?
Yes. New accounts get 5 GB of Standard storage and 100 GB of downloads per month free for 12 months.

Do you offer discounts?
Customers storing more than 500 TB can request volume pricing. Annual commitments receive a 15 percent discount.

LIMITS

What is the maximum object size?
A single object can be up to 5 TB. Uploads larger than 100 MB should use
multipart upload.

How many buckets can I create?
Each account can create up to 100 buckets by default. Contact support to raise the limit. Bucket names are unique
within a region and must be 3 to 63 characters long.

What is the request rate limit?
Each bucket supports at least 3,500 write requests and 5,500 read requests per second per prefix. Requests above
this rate receive an HTTP 503 response and should be retried with exponential backoff.

SECURITY

Is my data encrypted?
Yes. All data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.
Customers can bring their own encryption keys on the Enterprise plan.

How do I control access?
Use access policies attached to users, roles or buckets. Buckets are private by default. Public access requires an
explicit setting and a confirmation step. Access keys can be rotated at any time and we recommend rotation every 90 days.

Which compliance standards do you meet?
The service is audited for SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001. A signed data processing agreement is available for GDPR
customers on request.

RELIABILITY

What is the uptime guarantee?
The service level agreement guarantees 99.95 percent monthly uptime for the
Standard tier. Service credits are issued automatically if the target is missed.

How durable is my data?
Objects are designed for 99.999999999 percent (eleven nines) annual durability. Versioning can be enabled per bucket
to protect against accidental deletion; deleted versions are kept for 30 days.

How do backups work?
Enable cross-region replication to copy objects to a second region within 15 minutes. Lifecycle rules can move
objects to the Archive tier after a number of days that you choose.

SUPPORT

How do I contact support?
Standard plans receive email support with a first response within 1 business day. Enterprise plans get 24/7 phone
support with a 30-minute response for critical issues.

How do I cancel my account?
Delete all buckets, then close the account in the console under Billing. Charges stop at the end of the billing month,
and data is permanently erased 30 days after closure.

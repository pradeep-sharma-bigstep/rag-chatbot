# Acme Cloud — Travel and Expense Policy

Version 2.4. Owner: Finance (finance@acmecloud.example). Applies to all employees and contractors who
travel or buy on behalf of the company.

## Principles
Spend company money as you would your own. Choose reasonable options, keep receipts, and submit claims promptly.
If a rule here conflicts with local law, local law wins; tell Finance so the policy can be updated.

## Booking travel
All flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel desk.
Economy class is standard for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is
allowed for longer flights.

Book at least 14 days ahead where possible. Business class is only allowed with written approval from a vice
president, for example when travel is overnight and a working day follows the arrival. Rail is preferred over air for
journeys under 4 hours.

### Hotels
Hotel rates are capped at 180 USD per night in standard cities and 260 USD per night in New York, London, San
Francisco, Singapore, and Tokyo. Stay in a hotel from the travel desk's list when possible. Hotel upgrades, minibar
items and spa services are not reimbursed.

### Ground transport
Use public transport or a taxi from the airport. Car rental needs manager approval and must be the smallest class that
fits the group. Personal car mileage is reimbursed at 0.55 USD per kilometre. Parking and tolls are reimbursed with receipts.

## Daily allowance
The meal allowance is 50 USD per day for domestic travel and 75 USD per day
for international travel. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

If meals are provided (for example at a conference), reduce the allowance by 30 percent per provided meal. Tips of up
to 15 percent are reimbursed within the allowance. Client dinners are claimed separately: record the attendees' names
and the business purpose, with a limit of 90 USD per person.

## Submitting expenses
Submit expense claims in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense.
Every claim above 25 USD needs a receipt. Claims are paid with the next
monthly payroll after approval.

Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed. Managers approve claims within 5 working days; claims above 2,000 USD
also need approval from the department head. Foreign currency claims are converted at the rate of the date of the
expense. Lost receipts: complete the missing-receipt form; no more than 3 per year are accepted.

## Advances
Employees may request a travel advance of up to 80 percent of the estimated trip cost, at least 10 days before
departure. Unspent advances must be returned within 14 days of the end of the trip or are deducted from payroll.

## Company cards
Employees who travel more than twice a month can request a corporate card.
Personal purchases on a corporate card are not allowed.

Lost or stolen cards must be reported to Finance within 24 hours. Card statements are reconciled in the finance portal
by the 5th of every month. Repeated unreconciled transactions lead to the card being suspended.

## Non-reimbursable items
Fines and penalties, personal entertainment, gifts to colleagues over 50 USD, flight upgrades paid with loyalty points
used for personal gain, and childcare during trips are not reimbursed.

## Frequently asked questions
**Can I extend a business trip for a holiday?** Yes. Book the extra days yourself, and claim only the business
portion; the travel desk can split the booking.

**Who approves a hotel above the cap?** The department head, before booking.

**How are claims paid?** By bank transfer with the next monthly payroll run, which closes on the 20th.
